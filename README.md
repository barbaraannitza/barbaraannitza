#### 🧩 Como eu trabalho

- **Design System como contrato:** cores, tipos, espaços e movimento em um único arquivo de tokens, com tema claro e escuro e uma checagem automática que barra valores soltos. [Veja ao vivo](https://barbaraannitza.com/sistema).
- **IA como par, revisão antes do ar:** eu descrevo a decisão de design, a mudança vira um pull request com prévia, e só publico depois de revisar no celular e no computador.
- **Medir com privacidade:** métricas sem cookies e sem coletar mais dados do que preciso.

### Hi, I'm Bárbara 👋

**Product Designer** · Design Systems · Research · AI in the process

I design close to code. I like to understand the problem before the screens, turn design decisions into tokens the code reads, and measure what ships. I use AI as a tool, not as a replacement for judgment.

🌐 **Portfolio:** [barbaraannitza.com](https://barbaraannitza.com) (PT/EN)

- **Design System as a contract:** color, type, spacing and motion in a single token file, with light and dark themes and an automated check that blocks stray values. [See it live](https://barbaraannitza.com/sistema).
- **AI as a pair, review before shipping:** I describe the design decision, the change becomes a pull request with a preview, and I only ship after reviewing it on phone and desktop.
- **Measuring with privacy:** cookie-free analytics, collecting no more data than I need.

Research published at IEEE on design and engineering collaboration: CHIuXiD 2023 · CHILECON 2023 · ICIC 2024.
